About RP Finance

RP Finance is a professional virtual stock trading platform built for investors who want to learn, practice, and sharpen their strategy with real market data — without risking real money.

Our Mission

We believe everyone deserves access to professional-grade trading tools. RP Finance combines live market data, algorithmic strategies, and an intuitive interface so you can trade smarter — not harder.

What We Offer

  • Live stock quotes powered by Alpha Vantage API
  • Virtual portfolio — buy and sell without real money
  • Full trade history with PnL tracking
  • Algorithmic trading with Moving Average Crossover signals
  • Watchlist to monitor your favourite stocks
  • Market news and financial insights
  • Interactive stock charts via TradingView
  • Educational tutorials for all levels
  • RP Assistant — AI-powered chatbot

Tech Stack

Built with Python / Flask, SQLite, Alpha Vantage API, NewsAPI, Chart.js, and TradingView widgets. Hosted free on PythonAnywhere.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Information We Collect

We collect your username, hashed password, and virtual trading activity (portfolio holdings, watchlist, trade history). No real financial information is collected.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide RP Finance services — maintaining your account, portfolio, and trade history. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Data Storage

Data is stored in a SQLite database on PythonAnywhere servers. Sessions are stored server-side using Flask-Session with filesystem storage.

Third-Party APIs

We use Alpha Vantage for stock data and NewsAPI for market news. Your searches may be sent to these services. Please review their privacy policies for details.

Cookies

We use session cookies to keep you logged in. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

Your Rights

You may delete your account at any time. Contact us at support@rpfinance.com to request account deletion.
